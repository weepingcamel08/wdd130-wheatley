Our Mission

Have you ever wanted to share the gospel, but had no idea where to start? That's what we're here for! A Brighter Way is committed to giving people fun and interesting ideas to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Whether it be online, in person, or through other means! We take great inspiration from the Love, Share, and Invite page of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints.
